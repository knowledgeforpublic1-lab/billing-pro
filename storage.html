Billing Pro

Private storage for billing and reconciliation

Billing Pro helps authorised project users manage RA bills, contractor and material records, reconciliation, and Daily Progress reports.

The administrator can connect a Google Drive account for application data storage. The Google connection requests access only to specific files used with the application. Other project users sign in through Billing Pro; they do not receive access to the administrator's Google account.

Storage availability depends on the modules configured by the administrator. Firebase Firestore continues to hold authentication and coordination records. Where Drive storage is enabled, application snapshots are stored in the connected private Drive folder.
